2 October 2023

TTPA Executive Committee Meets at Tiruchirappalli

Executive Committee Meeting • Tiruchirappalli

The TTPA Executive Committee met at Tiruchirappalli under the chairmanship of President Dr. N. Natarajan. Dr. M. Babu welcomed the members, and General Secretary Dr. K. S. Palaniswami presented the agenda and the minutes of the previous meeting for confirmation.

Members Present

  1. Dr. N. Natarajan
  2. Dr. M. Babu
  3. Dr. K. S. Palaniswami
  4. Dr. S. A. Asokan
  5. Dr. M. Sekar
  6. Dr. L. Gunaseelan
  7. Dr. D. Sundararaj
  8. Dr. V. Balakrishnan
  9. Dr. S. Sivaselvam
  10. Dr. N. Punniamurthy

Important Proceedings

  • Guidelines were approved for voluntary donations and the establishment and administration of endowments.
  • The TTPA Excellence Award was proposed to recognise deserving TANUVAS students in academics, sports, games and other talents.
  • The objectives and format of the first quarterly TTPA Newsletter were presented by Editor Dr. V. Balakrishnan. The draft issue was released by the President and received by the Editor.
  • The handbook Veterinary Herbal Medicine, authored and compiled by Rtn. Prof. N. Punniamurthy, was put into circulation.
  • Financial accounts, investment arrangements, voluntary annual contributions and the proposed TTPA Directory 2023 were discussed.
  • Members discussed furnishing the TTPA office, NHIS and Life Certificate matters, and conducting alternate Executive Committee meetings outside Chennai.

The meeting concluded with obituary references, two minutes of silence in remembrance of departed pensioners, and a vote of thanks to the organisers.

3 February 2024

Building a Stronger Foundation for Welfare and Benevolent Service

Second Annual General Body Meeting • Conference Hall, Madras Veterinary College, Chennai

The Second Annual General Body Meeting of TTPA was held following the Alumni Meet and was attended by 47 members. Dr. N. Natarajan, President, welcomed the gathering and presided over the meeting.

The General Body reviewed the Association’s financial position and discussed strengthening its resources to support deserving veterinary students and provide assistance to pensioners during emergencies.

16 August 2024

Connecting Past, Present and Future: Third TTPA General Body Meeting at Namakkal

Third Annual General Body Meeting • Veterinary College and Research Institute, Namakkal

The Third Annual General Body Meeting of TTPA was held in the newly refurbished auditorium of Veterinary College and Research Institute, Namakkal. In the absence of the President, Dr. M. Babu, Vice-President, presided over the meeting.

Dr. M. Selvaraju, Dean, VC&RI, Namakkal, inaugurated the meeting. The transformation of the auditorium into a modern facility was presented, and the team responsible for its development was congratulated.

The annual report and audited financial statement for 2023–2024 were presented and approved. Members also discussed streamlining the Association’s banking arrangements and reviewed the conditions connected with University recognition of TTPA.

A subcommittee was constituted to examine the recognition order and submit recommendations to the Executive Committee. General pensioner grievances, audit-related matters and facilities for submitting Digital Life Certificates were also discussed.

The students and staff of VC&RI presented a cultural programme. The Dean and the local organising team were felicitated for their hospitality and successful conduct of the meeting.

After lunch, TTPA members interacted with students through a motivational programme titled “Connect Past, Present and Future.” The session enabled pensioners to share their professional experience with the younger generation.

Thirty-four pensioners, including two family pensioners, participated in the programme. The meeting concluded with a vote of thanks proposed by Dr. Cecilia Joseph and the National Anthem.

13 September 2025

Growing Together, Serving Better: Fourth TTPA Annual General Body Meeting

Fourth Annual General Body Meeting • Conference Hall, Madras Veterinary College, Chennai

Dr. N. Natarajan, President, presided over the Fourth Annual General Body Meeting. Sixty-three pensioners, including seven family pensioners, participated in the programme.

The gathering was enriched by the presence of senior nonagenarian members. The President appreciated the Association’s growth and thanked members for their sustained cooperation and participation.

The annual report and audited accounts were presented and approved. Members were informed that TTPA had grown to include 236 pensioners and 95 family pensioners.

The General Body reviewed assistance provided in pension, family-pension, health-insurance and life-certificate matters. The functioning of the Grievance Committee and its role in representing common pensioner concerns were also explained.

The successful implementation of the Digital Life Certificate was highlighted, with nearly 200 pensioners and family pensioners having used the facility. Its importance for senior members with mobility limitations was particularly recognised.

Dr. V. Balakrishnan, Editor, presented the progress of the bimonthly TTPA Newsletter and invited suggestions for improving its content and usefulness.

Dr. N. Daniel Joy Chandran was nominated as Returning Officer for the forthcoming selection of office-bearers. The General Body also welcomed the proposal to distribute a TTPA calendar to members.

The contributions and services of senior members were gratefully acknowledged. Following remembrance of departed members, the meeting concluded with a vote of thanks proposed by Dr. D. Kathiresan and the National Anthem.

Honouring Excellence, Embracing Innovation: Fifth TTPA Annual General Body Meeting

Fifth Annual General Body Meeting • Conference Hall, Dr. Bertie D’Souza Block, Madras Veterinary College, Chennai

Dr. N. Natarajan, President, presided over the Fifth Annual General Body Meeting. Eighty-four participants, including a family pensioner, attended the programme.

The General Body reviewed the growth and welfare activities of the Association. The renewal of TTPA’s registration was announced, and the minutes of the Fourth Annual General Body Meeting were confirmed.

The Association’s financial position and accounts were presented and approved. Members were reminded that contributions to TTPA are voluntary and support its welfare and service activities.

Padma Shri Dr. N. Punniamurthy, Dr. P. Thangaraju, Dr. P. Mathialagan and Dr. G. Dhinakar Raj were recognised for distinguished professional and public service.

The Star of the Year 2025 awards were conferred on Dr. V. Balakrishnan and Dr. P. Sriram in recognition of their dedicated and distinguished service to TTPA.

Nonagenarian and octogenarian members were felicitated in appreciation of their lifelong contributions to the veterinary profession and the Association.

Members were briefed on Digital Life Certificates, additional pension, family-pension communication and other welfare provisions. The importance of keeping family pensioners informed about TTPA activities was emphasised.

Dr. V. Balakrishnan presented plans to develop the bimonthly newsletter into a user-friendly multimedia platform with searchable archives, text-to-speech support and hyperlinks for easy navigation.

The Association thanked its sponsors, student volunteers and members whose support contributed to the successful conduct of the programme.

Following remembrance of departed pensioners and family pensioners, the meeting concluded with a vote of thanks proposed by Dr. P. Ramadass and the National Anthem.
