Golden Bonds at Fifty: MVC 1976 Batch Golden Jubilee Reunion

Batch: Madras Veterinary College 1976 Batch Event: Golden Jubilee Reunion Date: 20–22 July 2026 Venues: Madras Veterinary College and Mahabalipuram

The Madras Veterinary College 1976 Batch reunited in Chennai to commemorate fifty years of friendship, shared learning and professional service.

The reunion photographs include batchmates who served in TTPA, government departments and other organisations. The portraits below identify the TTPA members featured in the group photographs.
