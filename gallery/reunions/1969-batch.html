Families, Friendship and Nature: Vet69 Palakkad Get-Together

Batch: Madras Veterinary College 1969 Batch Event: Vet69 Batch Families' Get-Together Date: 16–18 July 2025 Venue: Palakkad

Members of the Madras Veterinary College 1969 Batch gathered with their families at Palakkad to renew cherished friendships and enjoy the serene beauty of the region's landscapes and reservoirs.

2–4 February 2026

Faith, Friendship and Fifty-Seven Years: MVC 1969 Batch Thanjavur Reunion

Batch: Madras Veterinary College 1969 Batch Event: Thanjavur Devotional Reunion Date: 2–4 February 2026 Venue: Thanjavur

The Madras Veterinary College 1969 Batch reunited at Thanjavur to commemorate fifty-seven years of friendship, shared learning and professional service. The batch took immense pride in felicitating Padma Shri Dr. N.Punniamurthy during their reunion.

The reunion photograph includes batchmates who served in TTPA, government departments and other organisations. The portraits below identify the TTPA members featured in the group photograph.
