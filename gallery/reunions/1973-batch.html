27–29 December 2014

Four Decades of Enduring Friendship: MVC 1973 Batch Yelagiri Reunion

Batch: Madras Veterinary College 1973 Batch Event: Forty-One Years Reunion Date: 27–29 December 2014 Venue: Yelagiri

The Madras Veterinary College 1973 Batch reunited at Yelagiri to commemorate forty-one years of friendship, shared learning and professional service.

The reunion photograph includes batchmates who served in TTPA, government departments and other organisations. The portraits below identify the TTPA members featured in the group photograph.
