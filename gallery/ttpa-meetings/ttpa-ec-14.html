TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners' Association Meeting

Executive Committee Meeting — 13 June 2026

Executive Committee Meeting info

13 June 2026

Strengthening Pensioner Support and Advancing Newsletter Initiatives

Bioinformatics Seminar Hall, Madras Veterinary College, Chennai

Dr. N. Natarajan, President, presided over the meeting and welcomed the recently retired members. The minutes of the Executive Committee meeting held on 26 April 2026 were confirmed.

The committee discussed measures to assist senior pensioners with life-certificate requirements, strengthen communication with family pensioners through the WhatsApp forum and complete the TTPA pensioners’ directory.

Members considered relocating the TTPA office to the renovated main building of Madras Veterinary College. Dr. D. Kathiresan was nominated as Chairman of the Grievance Committee.

A committee chaired by Dr. V. Balakrishnan, with Dr. L. Gunaseelan and Dr. P. Sriram as members, was constituted to recommend suitable recipients for the STAR of TTPA 2026 award.

Plans were outlined for a bilingual, graphics-supported newsletter featuring the contributions of distinguished former faculty. Medical-insurance guidance for pensioners will also be included in a forthcoming issue.

The financial position and audit of the Association were reviewed. The committee decided to consolidate its fixed deposits and savings account with Union Bank of India, Madras Veterinary College branch.

Recently retired members were felicitated. The meeting concluded with a vote of thanks proposed by Dr. S. Gomathinayagam.
