Executive Committee Meeting — 4 January 2025

Executive Committee Meeting info

4 January 2025

Reviewing AGM Outcomes and Advancing Pensioner Services

TTPA Executive Committee Meeting • Madras Veterinary College, Chennai

President Dr. N. Natarajan welcomed the Executive Committee members and reviewed the successful conduct of the Annual General Body Meeting at Veterinary College and Research Institute, Namakkal.

Members Present

  2. Dr. A. Koteeswaran

Important Proceedings

  • The Committee recorded its appreciation to Dr. M. Selvaraju, Dean, VCRI Namakkal, and the organising team for the successful conduct of the Annual General Body Meeting.
  • The faculty members and student volunteers of VCRI Namakkal were thanked for their support and hospitality.
  • Dr. Cecilia Joseph reported on the university’s recognition of TTPA and the need to seek suitable amendments to certain conditions in the recognition order.
  • Pension-related audit objections concerning Principal Investigators and schemes were considered for resolution in consultation with the University.
  • Secretary Dr. P. Sriram reported that the Association’s Income Tax Return had been filed and that documents were being assembled for renewal of the Association.
  • Treasurer Dr. M. Sekar presented the expenditure incurred for the Annual General Body Meeting, which was approved.
  • The Committee appreciated the services of Dr. S. Gomathinayagam and Dr. P. Sriram and approved reimbursement of expenses incurred for official TTPA work.
  • Progress towards implementation of the Digital Life Certificate facility was reviewed.

Dr. L. Gunaseelan, Executive Committee Member, proposed the vote of thanks.
