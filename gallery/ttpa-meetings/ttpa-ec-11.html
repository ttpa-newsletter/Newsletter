Executive Committee Meeting — 13 December 2025

Executive Committee Meeting info

13 December 2025

Planning Leadership Transition and Recognising Dedicated Service

TTPA Executive Committee Meeting • Bioinformatics Centre, Madras Veterinary College

The Executive Committee met at 10.00 a.m. in the Seminar Hall of the Bioinformatics Centre. President Dr. N. Natarajan presided, greeted the gathering and appreciated the members for the efficient functioning of the Association.

Members Present

  1. Dr. N. Natarajan
  2. Dr. K. S. Palaniswami
  3. Dr. P. Sriram
  4. Dr. S. A. Asokan
  5. Dr. L. Gunaseelan
  6. Dr. S. Gomathinayagam
  7. Dr. P. Ramadass
  8. Dr. D. Sundararaj
  9. Dr. G. Dhinakar Raj
  10. Dr. M. Sekar
  11. Dr. Cecilia Joseph

Special Invitees

  • Dr. R. Prabakaran
  • Dr. S. Thilagar
  • Dr. N. Daniel Joy Chandran

Important Proceedings

  • The Committee appreciated Dr. S. Thilagar for initiating the TTPA 2026 calendar and Dr. R. Prabakaran for designing and arranging its printing and distribution.
  • The General Secretary informed members that the tenure of the existing office-bearers would end on 23 March 2026 and outlined arrangements for selecting the office-bearers and Executive Committee for 2026–29.
  • Dr. N. Daniel Joy Chandran was appointed as the Returning Officer to receive expressions of willingness from members.
  • TTPA’s continuing assistance with pension settlements, service matters and unresolved audit objections was reviewed.
  • Dr. G. Dhinakar Raj was requested to pursue recognition of TTPA by the Government.
  • Treasurer Dr. M. Sekar presented the financial statement and was requested to remind members about annual voluntary contributions and outstanding amounts.
  • Progress concerning renewal of the Association was reviewed.
  • Dr. L. Gunaseelan was requested to explore the possibility of organising a family tour for TTPA members.
  • The President and General Secretary proposed honouring Dr. P. Sriram and Dr. V. Balakrishnan as “Stars of 2025” for their services to TTPA.

Remembrance

Two minutes of silence were observed in memory of members of the pensioners’ community and their families who had passed away.

Dr. S. A. Asokan proposed the vote of thanks. TTPA thanked Dr. D. Kathiresan for hosting lunch.
