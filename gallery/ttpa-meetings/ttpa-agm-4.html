TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners' Association Meetings

Fourth Annual General Body Meeting — 13 September 2025

13 September 2025

Growing Together, Serving Better: Fourth TTPA Annual General Body Meeting

Fourth Annual General Body Meeting • Conference Hall, Madras Veterinary College, Chennai

Dr. N. Natarajan, President, presided over the Fourth Annual General Body Meeting. Sixty-three pensioners, including seven family pensioners, participated in the programme.

The gathering was enriched by the presence of senior nonagenarian members. The President appreciated the Association’s growth and thanked members for their sustained cooperation and participation.

The annual report and audited accounts were presented and approved. Members were informed that TTPA had grown to include 236 pensioners and 95 family pensioners.

The General Body reviewed assistance provided in pension, family-pension, health-insurance and life-certificate matters. The functioning of the Grievance Committee and its role in representing common pensioner concerns were also explained.

The successful implementation of the Digital Life Certificate was highlighted, with nearly 200 pensioners and family pensioners having used the facility. Its importance for senior members with mobility limitations was particularly recognised.

Dr. V. Balakrishnan, Editor, presented the progress of the bimonthly TTPA Newsletter and invited suggestions for improving its content and usefulness.

Dr. N. Daniel Joy Chandran was nominated as Returning Officer for the forthcoming selection of office-bearers. The General Body also welcomed the proposal to distribute a TTPA calendar to members.

The contributions and services of senior members were gratefully acknowledged. Following remembrance of departed members, the meeting concluded with a vote of thanks proposed by Dr. D. Kathiresan and the National Anthem.
