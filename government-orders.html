Letter No. 7277 / Pension / 2018

21 February 2018

Department:
Finance (Pension) Department, Government of Tamil Nadu

Document Type:
Government Clarification Letter / Circular

Subject:
Pensioners / Family Pensioners – Request for clarification regarding Additional Pension and Additional Family Pension

At a Glance

This clarification addresses procedural delays in sanctioning additional pension and additional family pension to beneficiaries aged 80 years and above. Where the date of birth is not recorded in the original Pension Payment Order, age may be verified through accepted identity documents such as PAN Card, SSLC, Passport, Driving Licence, Voter ID or other accepted identity proof. It also provides for applications through Form 14, where applicable, to facilitate sanction without avoidable procedural delay.

Pension Policy

G.O.(Ms).No.165

31 May 2023

Department:
Finance (Pension) Department

Document Type:
Government Order (Ms)

Subject:
Pension / Family Pension – Simplification of mustering process – Tamil Nadu State Government Pensioners – Annual Mustering of Pensioners / Family Pensioners during the month of their retirement / commencement – Orders issued

At a Glance

The Government revised the annual mustering process for State Government pensioners and family pensioners. With effect from 1 July 2023, annual mustering is conducted during the month corresponding to the pensioner's retirement or commencement of family pension instead of restricting mustering to a fixed July–September period. A one-month grace period is provided before pension payment is stopped for non-mustering.

Pension Policy

G.O. (Ms) No. 42

7 February 2011

Department:
Finance (Pension) Department, Government of Tamil Nadu

Document Type:
Government Order (Ms)

Subject:
Pension / Family Pension – Sanction of Additional Pension and Additional Family Pension – Orders issued

At a Glance

This order sanctions additional pension and additional family pension for State Government pensioners and family pensioners aged 80 years and above, with monetary benefit from 1 January 2011. The additional pension is prescribed at 20% for 80–84 years, 30% for 85–89 years, 40% for 90–94 years, 50% for 95–99 years and 100% for persons aged 100 years and above. The order also contains provisions relating to age verification and financial accounting.

Health Insurance

G.O.Ms.No.123

24 June 2026

Department:
Finance [Health Insurance] Department

Document Type:
Government Order

Subject:
Implementation of the New Health Insurance Scheme, 2026 for Pensioners (including spouse) / Family Pensioners through United India Insurance Company Limited, Chennai

At a Glance

The order implements the New Health Insurance Scheme, 2026 for eligible pensioners and family pensioners for the five-year block period from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2031. It provides cashless healthcare assistance up to ₹7.50 lakh for approved treatments and surgeries and up to ₹12 lakh for specified enhanced illnesses on a floater basis. The annual premium is ₹7,728 per pensioner, with ₹644 recovered monthly from pension from July 2026. The scheme covers 2,992 approved treatments/surgeries through empanelled hospitals, subject to the provisions of the Government Order.

Health Insurance

G.O.Ms.No.204

30 June 2022

Department:
Finance [Health Insurance] Department

Document Type:
Government Order

Subject:
Medical Aid – New Health Insurance Scheme, 2022 for Pensioners (including spouse) / Family Pensioners – Provision of Health Care Assistance through United India Insurance Company Limited, Chennai – Implementation – Orders issued

At a Glance

This order implemented the New Health Insurance Scheme, 2022 for Tamil Nadu State pensioners and family pensioners for the four-year block period from 1 July 2022 to 30 June 2026. It provided cashless medical assistance up to ₹5 lakh generally and up to ₹10 lakh for specified critical illnesses. The scheme covered 203 approved procedures and 1,221 empanelled hospitals. The annual premium was ₹5,472 plus GST per pensioner, with a monthly recovery of ₹497 from pension beginning July 2022.
