About the Photographs in This Archive

Many of the photographs presented in this section have been collected from very old archival records and other available sources, some dating back more than six decades. In several instances, the only photograph available was one taken when the individual was much younger, sometimes even during their teenage years.

To preserve these cherished memories and make the photographs suitable for presentation in this digital archive, the available images have been reconstructed and enhanced with the assistance of Artificial Intelligence (AI). Every reasonable effort has been made to retain the individual's recognizable appearance and to present the person respectfully. Nevertheless, owing to the age, quality and limitations of the original source photographs, an AI-reconstructed image may not reproduce every facial feature or detail with complete accuracy.

We welcome corrections and better photographs. If a family member, colleague or other person familiar with the individual feels that a displayed photograph does not adequately represent the person, they are requested to inform the Editor in writing. On receiving such a request, the photograph can be withdrawn or replaced with a more appropriate photograph provided by the family or other reliable source.

Members are also warmly invited to share photographs of departed members whose images are presently unavailable or not displayed in this archive. Such contributions will help us progressively build a more complete and authentic visual record of those who were part of our institution and profession.
