Welcome to Our Pensioners’ Family

Celebrating distinguished careers and welcoming our newly retired colleagues to the TTPA family.

Dr. S. T. Selvan

Retired Professor and Head,
Department of Poultry Science,
Madras Veterinary College, Chennai

TTPA warmly welcomes and congratulates Dr. S. T. Selvan on his superannuation on 31 July 2026, following an impressive 33 years and 8 months of dedicated service .

Born on 7 July 1966, Dr. Selvan commenced his University service on 8 February 1993 at FTC, Hosur . During his distinguished career, he served in various research farms for more than 25 years, besides contributing five years to college teaching and academic activities.

He also served as Dean, College of Poultry Production and Management, Hosur, from 2021 to 2024 , providing valuable academic and administrative leadership for three years.

TTPA extends its warmest greetings and heartfelt wishes to Dr. Selvan for a happy, healthy and fulfilling retired life , filled with peace, joy and cherished moments.
