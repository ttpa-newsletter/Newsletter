EDITOR’S CORNER • 05

Newsletter Objectives

The purpose, scope, content framework and submission guidelines of the TTPA Multimedia Newsletter.

Objectives of the Newsletter

  • To disseminate the activities of the TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association.
  • To spread awareness about information relevant to senior citizens and old age.
  • To share achievements of pensioners or their wards that have received recognition from a third party during the period since the preceding edition.

Newsletter Format

  • Greeting message by the President or General Secretary.
  • Minutes of the Executive Council meeting.
  • Introduction of newcomers to the pensioners’ family.
  • A joyful rewind of memories and interactions with colleagues and students during members’ years as teachers.
  • New professional information.
  • Abstracts of geriatric tips for a healthy life.
  • Pensioner achievements during the reporting period.
  • Philanthropic activities of pensioners during the reporting period.
  • Laudable acts by pensioners.
  • Events organised by members at their respective locations.
  • Events in which members participate that are organised by an alma mater or by private, State, national or international organisations.
  • Tips on savings and financial security.
  • Reunion details with photographs.
  • Appreciation of a deceased colleague and obituary notices.
  • Letters to the Editor.

Articles Not Accepted for Publication

To preserve the purpose and character of the newsletter, the following categories of content are outside its ambit:

  • Appreciation or condemnation of a living pensioner.
  • Religious messages.
  • Political messages.
  • Grievances against the University or Government.

Article Submission

Members may send articles directly to the Editor through WhatsApp, together with relevant photographs wherever appropriate.

Contributors are requested to ensure that submissions remain within the guidelines and ambit of the newsletter outlined above.
