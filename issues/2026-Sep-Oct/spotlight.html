A Landmark Honour for Indian Veterinary Science

Dr. S. Balasubramanian Elected Honorary Member of ACT

TTPA extends its warmest congratulations to Dr. S. Balasubramanian on his election as an Honorary Member of the American College of Theriogenologists (ACT) , a prestigious international recognition for exceptional contributions to theriogenology—the field of veterinary reproduction.

ACT reserves this honour for individuals of unusual eminence who have made significant contributions to knowledge in veterinary reproduction. According to the announcement, Dr. Balasubramanian is the first veterinarian from India, and indeed from Asia, to receive this distinction , making it a landmark achievement for Indian veterinary science. In the 55-year history of ACT, only 28 individuals have reportedly received this rare honour.

The recognition reflects Dr. Balasubramanian’s distinguished contributions to education, research, clinical practice and professional leadership .

TTPA proudly congratulates Dr. S. Balasubramanian on this outstanding international honour and wishes him continued success and greater laurels in the years ahead.

Inspiring the Next Generation of Veterinarians

Padma Shri Dr. N. Punniamurthy Delivers an Inspiring Graduation Day Address

Padma Shri Dr. N. Punniamurthy delivered an inspiring and thought-provoking address at the Graduation Day function held at the Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Veterinary Education and Research on 23 August 2026 .

His address highlighted the responsibilities of graduating veterinarians in serving society, safeguarding animal health, ensuring food security and contributing to sustainable agriculture . He emphasised continuous learning, professional ethics, compassion towards animals and the importance of maintaining strong connections with farmers and the community.

His message encouraged the young graduates to view veterinary medicine not merely as a profession, but as a noble service carrying immense social responsibility .

TTPA warmly congratulates Dr. Punniamurthy for his meaningful address, which undoubtedly encouraged the graduates to pursue their careers with commitment, integrity, empathy and dedication to the welfare of animals and humanity.

Padma Shri Dr. N. Punniamurthy Felicitated at His Alma Mater

Padma Shri Dr. N. Punniamurthy was felicitated at Madras Veterinary College, TANUVAS , in honour of receiving the Padma Shri for his pioneering contributions to ethno-veterinary medicine .

The programme served as both an alumni felicitation and an academic tribute recognising the conferment of this prestigious national honour. Several alumni, including former Vice-Chancellor Dr. P. Thangaraju , paid tribute to him. On behalf of the 1975 B.V.Sc. batch, he was honoured in a traditional manner.

The occasion celebrated his four-decade-long career in developing standardised herbal remedies for common cattle ailments, with emphasis on reducing antibiotic use and the cost of dairy production.

Addressing faculty, researchers and students, Dr. Punniamurthy spoke about integrating traditional herbal knowledge with rigorous clinical validation . The University community lauded his commitment to empowering rural farmers through sustainable livestock healthcare and for bringing national recognition to his alma mater.

A Lifelong Champion of Sports and Sportsmanship

Dr. A. M. Venkatesan , former Deputy Director of Physical Education, continued his remarkable association with University sports through a series of engagements during June–August 2026.

He delivered felicitation addresses at the Annual Sports Days of VCRI, Namakkal (23 June) and VCRI, Orathanadu (3 July) . He served as Guest of Honour at Madras Veterinary College, Chennai (30 June) and VCRI, Salem (13 July) , where he also addressed the gathering. He was a Special Invitee at VCRI, Tirunelveli (9 July) .

Continuing his active involvement, Dr. Venkatesan was invited as a Patron of the Veterinary Premier League at Coimbatore on 27 August 2026 , involving cricket competition among 16 teams.

Beyond his active participation, Dr. Venkatesan has generously sponsored several medals to encourage sporting excellence among students. His sustained involvement reflects his enduring passion for physical education and the nurturing of young talent.

TTPA heartily congratulates Dr. A. M. Venkatesan for his exemplary contribution to promoting sportsmanship across TANUVAS institutions.

TANUVAS Honours Inventors for Patented Innovations

As part of its 80th Independence Day celebrations , TANUVAS honoured its inventors for their valuable contributions to innovation and technology development.

Dr. K. Kumanan, Dr. G. Dhinakar Raj and Dr. K. Vijayarani received Certificates of Appreciation for their patent, “Multiplex primers for detection and determination of Mycobacterium orygis” (Patent No. 592827), granted by the Government of India on 19 June 2026.

Dr. S. Thilagar, Dr. R. Jayaprakash, Dr. S. Balasubramanian and Dr. Cecilia Joseph were similarly recognised for developing the patented technology, “An Animal Ambulance Unit and a Method of Shifting Sick Recumbent Animal for Transportation” , granted on 10 April 2024.

TTPA heartily congratulates both teams for translating scientific expertise into valuable innovations benefiting veterinary diagnostics and animal healthcare.
