Welcome to Our Pensioners’ Family

Celebrating distinguished careers and welcoming our newly retired colleagues to the TTPA family.

Dr. M. Parthiban

Retired Professor and Head,
Department of Animal Biotechnology,
Madras Veterinary College, TANUVAS

TTPA warmly welcomes and congratulates Dr. M. Parthiban on his superannuation after an illustrious academic career spanning more than 32 years.

Dr. Parthiban has made significant contributions to teaching, molecular virology, diagnostics and animal biotechnology . His distinguished research record includes 130 research publications, guidance of Ph.D., M.V.Sc. and M.Phil. scholars , leadership of externally funded projects, two patents, and the development of a diagnostic kit for avian oncogenic viruses .

His service as Course Director, committee member and Dean (in charge) further reflects his academic and administrative leadership.

TTPA extends its heartfelt greetings and warmest wishes to Dr. Parthiban for a happy, healthy and fulfilling retired life . May this new chapter bring him abundant joy, peace and cherished moments with family and friends.
