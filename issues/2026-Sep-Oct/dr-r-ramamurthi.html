Academic Journey and Leadership

Dr. R. Ramamurthi, M.V.Sc., Ph.D., was born on 1 October 1933 to Mrs. R. Devakiammal and Mr. R. N. Ethirajulu. He completed his B.V.Sc. at Madras Veterinary College and subsequently pursued his M.V.Sc., with Meat and Milk Hygiene as his main subject, completing the degree in 1964. He later obtained his Ph.D. in Poultry Science in 1985.

He headed the Department of Meat Science and Technology from 3 August 1978 to 15 April 1986. He was subsequently appointed as Dean of the Veterinary College and Research Institute, Namakkal and retired on 1 March 1989.

Contributions to Teaching and Research

Dr. Ramamurthi made significant contributions to postgraduate education and research. He guided as many as 11 M.V.Sc. and two Ph.D. scholars, contributing to the development of specialists in his field.

He was a long-standing member of the FAD 18, Slaughterhouse and Meat Industry Sectional Committee, initially serving as an alternate member and subsequently as a primary member. He also served on one of the boards of the Central Food Technological Research Institute.

His academic expertise was recognised by several universities and institutions. He served as a question paper setter, including for the Indian Council of Agricultural Research, and evaluated Master's and Doctoral theses from various universities.

A Legacy Remembered

Dr. Ramamurthi passed away on 29 August 1989. His contributions to veterinary education continue to be remembered. Academic Block III of the Veterinary College and Research Institute, Namakkal, was named the Dr. R. Ramamurthi Block in his honour.

His son, Mr. R. Prabhakar, instituted the Dr. R. Ramamurthi Endowment for the Best Outgoing Ph.D. scholar in Livestock Products Technology (Meat Science), thereby perpetuating his father's memory and commitment to academic excellence.

With Condolences

His wife, Smt. R. Bhargavi, passed away on 8 August 2026 after a brief illness. TTPA respectfully offers its heartfelt condolences to the bereaved family.
