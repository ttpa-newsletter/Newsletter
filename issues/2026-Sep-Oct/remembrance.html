SECTION 12

In Fond Remembrance

Remembering with gratitude those whose lives, service and association remain close to our hearts.

Dr. Md. Mustaq Ahamed

Dr. Md. Mustaq Ahamed

We deeply mourn the sad demise of Dr. Md. Mustaq Ahamed, former Professor and Head, Department of Veterinary Science, Madras Veterinary College, who passed away on 3 July 2026.

He was one of the distinguished non-veterinarians who dedicated himself wholeheartedly to the development of Madras Veterinary College. As Professor of Biochemistry at CCL, MVC, and later as Head of Biochemistry under the Department of Physiology, he rendered exemplary academic and administrative service.

His expertise in independently managing Biochemistry teaching for both undergraduate and postgraduate students was exceptional. He ensured well-equipped practical laboratories with adequate infrastructure and consumables, thereby setting high standards in practical education.

His meticulous training of laboratory technicians, including the preparation of handwritten SOP-style practical notes, remains an enduring lesson for generations of teachers.

His contributions to Madras Veterinary College will always be remembered with gratitude and respect.

Dr. S. Baegan

Dr. S. Baegan

We mourn the sad demise of Dr. S. Baegan, who passed away on 15 July 2026.

He was an excellent human being, a good Samaritan, and a calm, humble and approachable person.

He played a silent yet vital role in shaping the TANUVAS campus at Madhavaram Milk Colony, working closely with former Director Dr. Venugopal and other officials. His valuable personal contacts at the Secretariat greatly helped the University in securing and transferring land for several centres.

He also contributed significantly to the World Bank-assisted TNAHRDP project, facilitating major infrastructure development and overseas training programmes.

A man of honesty and conviction, he strongly advocated CAS promotions for TANUVAS Veterinary Faculty on par with TNAU teaching staff.

His invaluable contributions and support will always be remembered by TANUVAS.

Dr. K. M. Selvaraj

Dr. K. M. Selvaraj

We deeply mourn the sad demise of Dr. K. M. Selvaraj, Reader, Department of Extension Education, Madras Veterinary College, and former Deputy Warden of the MVC Hostel, who passed away on 22 August 2026 after a brief illness.

Dr. Selvaraj was a dedicated and compassionate teacher who extended his commitment to education beyond the classroom, reaching students as well as the general public.

Through his writings and thoughtful social media contributions, he consistently used his knowledge and experience for the betterment of society. His service, simplicity and helping nature earned him the respect and affection of many.

In keeping with his noble and selfless spirit, he had expressed his wish to donate his body to Madras Medical College for medical education and the training of future generations of doctors.

This extraordinary final gift reflects the compassion, generosity and spirit of service that defined his life.

Smt. R. Bhargavi

Smt. R. Bhargavi

We deeply mourn the sad demise of Smt. R. Bhargavi, aged 81 years, beloved wife of late Dr. R. Ramamurthy and mother of Mr. Prabakaran, who passed away peacefully on 8 August 2026 after a brief illness.

Her passing is a profound loss to her family, relatives and all those who knew and cherished her. We extend our heartfelt condolences to the bereaved family and pray that they may find strength and courage to overcome this difficult hour.

TTPA deeply condoles the sad demise of Smt. R. Bhargavi and conveys its heartfelt condolences to Mr. Prabakaran and the bereaved family. May her soul rest in peace.
