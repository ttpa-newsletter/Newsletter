Pensioners in the Spotlight

Celebrating honours, achievements, professional contributions and continuing service by members of our pensioners’ community.

A Lifelong Champion of Sports and Sportsmanship

Dr. A. M. Venkatesan , former Deputy Director of Physical Education, continued his remarkable association with University sports through a series of engagements during June–August 2026.

He delivered felicitation addresses at the Annual Sports Days of VCRI, Namakkal (23 June) and VCRI, Orathanadu (3 July) . He served as Guest of Honour at Madras Veterinary College, Chennai (30 June) and VCRI, Salem (13 July) , where he also addressed the gathering. He was a Special Invitee at VCRI, Tirunelveli (9 July) .

Continuing his active involvement, Dr. Venkatesan was invited as a Patron of the Veterinary Premier League at Coimbatore on 27 August 2026 , involving cricket competition among 16 teams.

Beyond his active participation, Dr. Venkatesan has generously sponsored several medals to encourage sporting excellence among students. His sustained involvement reflects his enduring passion for physical education and the nurturing of young talent.

TTPA heartily congratulates Dr. A. M. Venkatesan for his exemplary contribution to promoting sportsmanship across TANUVAS institutions.
