EDITOR’S CORNER • 04

Acknowledgement

With sincere appreciation to all who contributed to this issue of the TTPA Multimedia Newsletter.

The preparation of this issue of the TTPA Multimedia Newsletter has been made possible through the encouragement, cooperation and contributions of many members and well-wishers.

I gratefully acknowledge all those who shared articles, reminiscences, photographs, documents, news items, professional achievements, family milestones and other material for inclusion in this issue.

I also thank those who offered suggestions during the development of the multimedia format and helped in testing its navigation, readability and interactive features.

The continued support of the Office Bearers and members of TTPA has been invaluable in shaping this initiative into a useful, accessible and evolving platform for our pensioner community.

Above all, my sincere thanks go to every reader who explores the newsletter, shares feedback and contributes towards preserving our collective professional memories for future generations.

With gratitude to all contributors and well-wishers.

Dr. V. Balakrishnan
Newsletter Editor & Website Administrator, TTPA
