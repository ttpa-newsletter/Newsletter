Distinguished Voices in Webinars

Promoting professional learning, international collaboration and contemporary veterinary knowledge.

Advancing One Health and Companion Animal Care Through Global Webinars

Captain (Dr.) G. Dhanan Jaya Rao is warmly congratulated for his leadership as Secretary General, Veterinary Association of India, in organising two timely international webinars.

World Zoonoses Day Webinar — 6 July 2026

The World Zoonoses Day programme highlighted veterinarians’ frontline role in zoonoses control and preparedness against emerging and re-emerging diseases, reinforcing the vital One Health approach to safeguarding animal and public health.

International Dog Day Webinar — 26 August 2026

The International Dog Day webinar addressed canine heartworm removal and advances in cardiopulmonary resuscitation, promoting contemporary companion-animal clinical care.

By bringing international experts together, these initiatives strengthen professional knowledge and its practical application to animal and public health.

TTPA heartily congratulates Captain (Dr.) G. Dhanan Jaya Rao on these valuable initiatives and wishes him continued success in advancing veterinary knowledge and professional development.
