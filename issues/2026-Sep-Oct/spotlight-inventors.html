TANUVAS Honours Inventors for Patented Innovations

As part of its 80th Independence Day celebrations , TANUVAS honoured its inventors for their valuable contributions to innovation and technology development.

Dr. K. Kumanan, Dr. G. Dhinakar Raj and Dr. K. Vijayarani received Certificates of Appreciation for their patent, “Multiplex primers for detection and determination of Mycobacterium orygis” (Patent No. 592827), granted by the Government of India on 19 June 2026.

Dr. S. Thilagar, Dr. R. Jayaprakash, Dr. S. Balasubramanian and Dr. Cecilia Joseph were similarly recognised for developing the patented technology, “An Animal Ambulance Unit and a Method of Shifting Sick Recumbent Animal for Transportation” , granted on 10 April 2024.

TTPA heartily congratulates both teams for translating scientific expertise into valuable innovations benefiting veterinary diagnostics and animal healthcare.
