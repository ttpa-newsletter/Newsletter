Activities of TTPA at a Glance

Recent activities, initiatives and member welfare measures.

TTPA continued its efforts to assist pensioners and family pensioners in matters relating to pensionary benefits, health insurance, life certificates and other welfare measures.

Digital Life Certificates

Digital Life Certificates were generated for 39 pensioners during the two-month period.

Facilitation of Family Pension and LTA

TTPA followed up cases relating to family pension and LTA. Family pension for the spouses of Dr. S. A. Joseph and Dr. Baegan was sanctioned at the earliest possible time.

Settlement of Pensionary Benefits

Pensionary benefits of all recent retirees have been settled except in the cases of Dr. Karunakaran and Dr. Parthiban. These two pending pension cases are under active follow-up and are expected to be settled shortly. PPO numbers have also been allotted to all recent retirees except these two members.

Follow-up with the University

The Secretary, accompanied by Dr. S. Gomathinayagam, visited the University twice and met the Finance Officer and the Registrar regarding the New Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS) , issue of NHIS cards, sanction of additional pension and family pension.

Sanction of Additional Pension

Two members received additional pension during this period on completion of 80 years of age.

Pensioners’ Family Security Fund

Pensioners’ Family Security Fund benefits in respect of all deceased members concerned were settled.

Follow-up of Members’ Grievances

A Special Executive Committee Meeting was convened on 12 August 2026 to consider and follow up the grievances of members. The minutes of the meeting are presented separately in this issue.

Form 16 – Income Tax

For the first time, Form 16 was obtained for all concerned members, including those from whom no tax had been deducted at source (TDS), and copies were sent digitally to their email addresses.
