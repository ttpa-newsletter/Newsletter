SECTION 01 • WELCOME NOTE

General Secretary’s Message

A Living Digital Archive of Our Shared Heritage

Dr. K. S. Palaniswami

General Secretary, TTPA

Dear Members,

The launch of our web-based TTPA Multimedia Newsletter marks an important milestone in the evolution of our Association’s communication.

Unlike a conventional newsletter that primarily records current events, the web edition provides a permanent, easily accessible and continuously expandable platform where information, memories, photographs and the history of our veterinary fraternity can be preserved for generations.

Preserving Our Shared Heritage

A particularly significant feature is our Archives. It presently brings together about 135 photographs, many representing teachers, colleagues and personalities associated with our institutions over several decades.

More than 100 of these photographs have been painstakingly reconstructed and enhanced with the assistance of Artificial Intelligence from records dating back nearly five decades. Browsing through these familiar faces will, I am sure, evoke cherished memories of our student days, teachers, colleagues and professional life.

Members Can Help Enrich the Archives

Members are warmly invited to contribute to this collective endeavour. If you possess photographs of our teachers or colleagues, irrespective of their present condition, please share them with TTPA.

Even faded, damaged or low-resolution photographs may be useful. Our Editor will endeavour to reconstruct and enhance suitable images and add them to the Archives.

Protecting Personal Information

As our newsletter is now available through a public web platform, due importance must also be given to safeguarding members’ personal information.

With this consideration, the Birthday column will henceforth be discontinued from the web-based newsletter. Members need not, however, miss Association updates and personal greetings.

We earnestly appeal to all TANUVAS teacher pensioners who have not yet joined the TANUVAS Teacher Pensioners Forum WhatsApp Group to become members of the group.

It provides a convenient forum for receiving timely TTPA updates, announcements and information of interest to our pensioner community while avoiding unnecessary publication of personal information on a public website.

Memories of Madras Veterinary College

Another fascinating component of the Archives is the collection of old photographs of buildings of Madras Veterinary College.

For generations of alumni and former faculty, these images are more than architectural records. Familiar classrooms, departments and campus landmarks can rekindle memories and take us back to an important period of our academic and professional lives.

A Living Digital Memorial

Thus, our web-based newsletter is gradually becoming much more than a medium for reporting Association activities.

It is evolving into a living digital memorial — preserving our people, places, achievements and institutional heritage while reconnecting generations of the TANUVAS veterinary fraternity.

Appreciation to the Editor

I place on record my sincere appreciation and congratulations to our Newsletter Editor & Website Administrator, Dr. V. Balakrishnan, for conceiving and single-handedly building this remarkable digital memorial.

His painstaking effort in collecting, reconstructing, organising and presenting these valuable archival materials deserves our wholehearted appreciation.

An Invitation to Every Member

I invite every member to visit the website, explore the Archives, relive treasured memories and, above all, contribute photographs and information so that this shared repository becomes richer and more complete with time.

Dr. K. S. Palaniswami
General Secretary, TTPA
