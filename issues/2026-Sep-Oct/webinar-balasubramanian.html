Dr. S. Balasubramanian Promotes Global Veterinary Knowledge Exchange

Dr. S. Balasubramanian is warmly congratulated for organising the Global Veterinary Clinical Sciences Webinar 2026 on 18 July 2026, featuring Prof. Dr. Lionel James Dawson of Oklahoma State University, USA.

The webinar focused on “Review of Assisted Reproductive Technologies Available in Small Ruminants”, a highly relevant subject for contemporary veterinary practice. Assisted reproductive technologies can accelerate genetic improvement, enhance reproductive efficiency, facilitate the dissemination of superior germplasm and support sustainable sheep and goat production.

Such programmes provide veterinarians with valuable exposure to global expertise and emerging clinical technologies while strengthening continuing veterinary education.

Excellent Participation: The webinar attracted 726 registrations, with 638 participants attending online, representing approximately 88% attendance.

TTPA heartily congratulates Dr. S. Balasubramanian for organising this academically valuable international webinar and wishes him continued success in promoting professional knowledge exchange.
