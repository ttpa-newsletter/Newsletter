A Landmark Honour for Indian Veterinary Science

Dr. S. Balasubramanian Elected Honorary Member of ACT

TTPA extends its warmest congratulations to Dr. S. Balasubramanian on his election as an Honorary Member of the American College of Theriogenologists (ACT) , a prestigious international recognition for exceptional contributions to theriogenology—the field of veterinary reproduction.

ACT reserves this honour for individuals of unusual eminence who have made significant contributions to knowledge in veterinary reproduction. According to the announcement, Dr. Balasubramanian is the first veterinarian from India, and indeed from Asia, to receive this distinction , making it a landmark achievement for Indian veterinary science. In the 55-year history of ACT, only 28 individuals have reportedly received this rare honour.

The recognition reflects Dr. Balasubramanian’s distinguished contributions to education, research, clinical practice and professional leadership .

TTPA proudly congratulates Dr. S. Balasubramanian on this outstanding international honour and wishes him continued success and greater laurels in the years ahead.
