Editor’s Message

Reflections from the Editor on this issue and the evolving TTPA Multimedia Newsletter.

Editor’s Message: Explore, Participate and Help Us Build

Dr. V. Balakrishnan

Newsletter Editor & Website Administrator, TTPA

Dear Readers,

Welcome to the TTPA Multimedia Newsletter, conceived not merely as another edition of our newsletter, but as a dynamic, interactive and continuously expanding digital platform for our pensioner community. It brings together Association activities, reminiscences of our stalwarts, achievements of pensioners, professional and social events, useful pension-related information, tributes, historical records and other features in an easily navigable format.

Explore the Archives

I particularly invite you to explore the Archives, which is intended to become a valuable permanent repository. Apart from reminiscences and historical material, please visit its practical subsections—Downloadable Forms, Procedures and Government Orders. These sections are being developed to enable pensioners to readily obtain commonly required forms, understand procedures for claiming various benefits and access relevant Government Orders from one convenient location.

The Archives also contain a growing collection of photographs of our teachers, colleagues and administrators, including many reconstructed from nearly five-decade-old records with the assistance of AI. Equally nostalgic are photographs of the old buildings of Madras Veterinary College. They are intended not simply to record structures, but to rekindle memories of the departments, classrooms, teachers, colleagues and events associated with them.

Help Us Enrich the Historical Record

This project, however, is still evolving. A few sections are yet to be completed. For example, the information presently given about the activities associated with each historical building is only brief. Those who studied or worked in these buildings may remember important departments, personalities, events, changes or anecdotes that deserve to be recorded. Please send your inputs so that these descriptions can be amplified and made historically richer.

Similarly, sections such as Gallery, Videos and PDF Edition are yet to be launched and will be progressively developed.

A Familiar Voice Behind Every Article

A particularly exciting feature planned for the multimedia newsletter is AI-assisted voice cloning. An author who contributes an article may provide the Editor with a short sample of his or her voice, conveniently recorded on a mobile phone. With the author’s consent, AI can use that sample to generate an audio narration of the entire article in a voice resembling that of the author.

Imagine reading a reminiscence while simultaneously hearing it narrated in the familiar voice of the colleague who wrote it. Besides adding warmth and personal connection to our newsletter, this facility can be especially helpful to senior readers who experience difficulty reading lengthy text on a screen. The audio option can therefore make our newsletter both more personal and more accessible.

About Reconstructed Photographs

If any photograph presently displayed in the Archives does not adequately reflect the person’s actual appearance, members are encouraged to provide a better photograph so that it can be replaced.

We should, however, remember that many reconstructed photographs originate from records nearly 50 years old. They are therefore intended to portray the individual as he or she appeared at that point in time, rather than at the time of retirement or in later years.

Your Participation Matters

This newsletter can become truly comprehensive only through collective participation. Photographs, old documents, Government Orders, forms, procedural information, historical details, corrections, reminiscences and suggestions are all welcome. Even seemingly small pieces of information may help complete an important part of our institutional history.

Finally, I invite every visitor to record your views and suggestions under the respective sections. Your responses will help me gauge the usefulness of individual features, identify areas requiring improvement and determine what should receive greater attention as the website develops.

Please explore beyond the home page, revisit the Archives, listen as multimedia features become available, contribute to filling the gaps, and help us transform this initiative into a lasting repository of our collective memories and useful information.

Wishing you an informative, nostalgic and enjoyable experience.

Dr. V. Balakrishnan
Newsletter Editor & Website Administrator, TTPA
