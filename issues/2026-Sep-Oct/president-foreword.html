SECTION 01 • WELCOME NOTE

Foreword

From the President, TTPA

Dr. N. Natarajan

President, TTPA

Dear Members and Friends,

It gives me immense pleasure to present the September–October 2026 issue of the TTPA Multimedia Newsletter, which marks another important step in the continuing evolution of our Association’s communication with its members.

What began as a simple newsletter in 2023 has steadily grown into a web-based multimedia platform. I place on record my appreciation of our Newsletter Editor and Website Administrator, Dr. V. Balakrishnan, for undertaking the uphill task of conceiving, developing and transforming the newsletter into its present form. This pioneering initiative brings together information, memories, archival material and interactive features in a format that is readily accessible to our pensioners.

Search, Review and Participate

A particularly useful feature of the new platform is the provision for searching within individual sections, enabling readers to locate information more conveniently. Equally important is the review and feedback facility associated with the sections.

I earnestly request our pensioners to register their views and suggestions. Your responses will help us assess how effectively the newsletter is serving our members and guide us in exploring further improvements.

An Engaging Brain Teaser

Another interesting innovation is the Brain Teaser, which uses images familiar to our veterinary fraternity and challenges readers to identify subtle differences. Besides providing a pleasant diversion, this interactive feature encourages observation, alertness and mental engagement.

Blessings to Our Octogenarians

This issue also gives me the privilege of conveying my affectionate greetings and blessings to our respected senior members who crossed the octogenarian milestone during September–October 2026:

Their long association with our profession and fraternity is a source of happiness and inspiration to all of us. On behalf of TTPA, I wish them good health, happiness, peace and many more fulfilling years ahead.

Moving Forward Together

May this multimedia initiative continue to bring our pensioners closer, preserve our cherished professional memories and provide a meaningful platform for information, interaction and fellowship.

I invite every member to explore the newsletter, participate in its interactive features and contribute towards its continued enrichment.

Greetings and best wishes to all our members and their families.

Dr. N. Natarajan
President, TTPA
