Called to Lead Once More

Distinguished members continuing to inspire, guide and contribute to the veterinary profession through leadership and professional engagement.

Dr. S. Prathaban Felicitated for Distinguished Contributions

TTPA warmly congratulates Dr. S. Prathaban , Professor, Dean and Director of Clinics, TANUVAS, on being felicitated at the 3rd Annual Symposium of the Indian Society for Veterinary Cardiology , held at Veterinary College, Bengaluru, in August 2026.

Recognised for his distinguished contributions to veterinary medicine, particularly veterinary cardiology and advanced diagnostic modalities , Dr. Prathaban has earned wide acclaim as a clinician, teacher and academic leader.

He was also invited to deliver the theme paper at the symposium, reflecting his expertise and professional standing. His sustained contributions to clinical practice, teaching, research and professional advancement have enriched veterinary education and animal healthcare.

TTPA takes pride in Dr. Prathaban’s achievement and wishes him continued success in advancing veterinary clinical sciences .

Dr. R. Jayaprakash Urges Delhi-Based TANUVAS Alumni to Build a Stronger Network

TTPA extends its warmest congratulations to Dr. R. Jayaprakash on his distinguished participation in the TANUVAS Alumni Meet – Delhi (NCR) 2026 .

His participation as a speaker reflected his continuing commitment to strengthening alumni bonds and fostering meaningful professional interaction among TANUVAS alumni.

By sharing his knowledge and experience, Dr. Jayaprakash encouraged fellow alumni to build a stronger network and contributed meaningfully to the spirit of lifelong learning, professional collaboration and alumni fellowship .

His continued involvement with the alumni community demonstrates the valuable role experienced professionals can play in bringing generations of TANUVAS graduates together and strengthening their connection with the University.

TTPA warmly appreciates Dr. Jayaprakash’s contribution and wishes him continued success in his efforts to strengthen and inspire the TANUVAS alumni community.
