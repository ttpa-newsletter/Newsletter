In Fond Remembrance

Remembering with gratitude those whose lives, service and association remain close to our hearts.

Dr. R. Ganesh

Dr. R. Ganesh

Dr. R. Ganesh, M.V.Sc., Ph.D., former Professor of Veterinary Surgery at Madras Veterinary College, TANUVAS, passed away on 14 March 2026 in Chennai.

Dr. Ganesh began his professional career with the Tamil Nadu Department of Animal Husbandry before joining academia at TANUVAS, where he served for many years. His academic and clinical work encompassed Veterinary Surgery and Radiology, Clinics, and the Resident Veterinary Services Section (RVSS), with particular involvement in radiology and large-animal surgery.

As a teacher and clinician, Dr. Ganesh was known among colleagues and students for his practical approach to teaching and patient care. Through his years of service, he contributed to veterinary education, clinical training and the professional development of students.

His association with Madras Veterinary College and his contributions to the veterinary profession will be remembered by his colleagues, students and friends.

TTPA expresses its profound sorrow at the passing of Dr. R. Ganesh and extends heartfelt condolences to his family and loved ones. May his soul rest in peace.
