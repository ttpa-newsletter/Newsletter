Distinguished Voices in Webinars

Promoting professional learning, international collaboration and contemporary veterinary knowledge.

Padma Shri Dr. N. Punniamurthy Participates in National Online Webinar

Padma Shri Dr. N. Punniamurthy participated in a National Online Webinar held from 2–8 April 2026, contributing his experience in ethno-veterinary medicine to the academic deliberations.

During the webinar, Dr. Punniamurthy shared perspectives on sustainable animal healthcare practices and discussed the relevance of traditional knowledge systems in contemporary veterinary science. His contribution highlighted approaches that draw upon field-based knowledge while addressing animal health and welfare.

The webinar provided a platform for veterinarians, researchers and students to exchange knowledge and perspectives on animal healthcare, including the role of sustainable and environmentally responsible approaches in veterinary practice.

TTPA congratulates Padma Shri Dr. N. Punniamurthy on his participation in the National Online Webinar and his continuing contribution to the sharing of professional knowledge in ethno-veterinary medicine.
