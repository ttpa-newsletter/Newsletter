Welcome to Our Pensioners' Family

Welcoming a new member to the TTPA pensioners' family.

Dr. Thangarajan Thamilvanan

TTPA warmly welcomes Dr. Thangarajan Thamilvanan to the pensioners' family following his retirement from TANUVAS.

During his professional career, Dr. Thamilvanan served the University in teaching, research and academic responsibilities and contributed to veterinary education and institutional activities.

TTPA wishes him a happy, healthy and fulfilling retired life and looks forward to his continued association with the pensioners' community.
