Dr. R. Asha Rajini Conferred Prince Award 2026

Dr. R. Asha Rajini

Dr. R. Asha Rajini was conferred the Prince Award 2026 by the Prince Group of Institutions, recognising her achievements and sustained commitment to an active life.

The recognition adds another milestone to Dr. Asha Rajini’s journey of fitness, perseverance and personal achievement. Her continued participation in physical activities reflects the value of maintaining an active lifestyle and pursuing personal goals beyond professional life.

Her achievement is particularly relevant to the spirit of “Athletes Are Made, Not Born,” demonstrating how commitment, regular effort and perseverance can help sustain an active life across the years.

TTPA congratulates Dr. R. Asha Rajini on receiving the Prince Award 2026 and wishes her continued success in her future pursuits.

Dr. R. Asha Rajini Wins First Prize in MEPZ Half Marathon

Dr. R. Asha Rajini

Dr. R. Asha Rajini secured First Prize in the Half Marathon (Female – 60+ Category) at the MEPZ Run, receiving a cash award of ₹20,000.

Competing in the senior women’s category, Dr. R. Asha Rajini demonstrated endurance, perseverance and commitment to an active lifestyle. Completing a half marathon is itself a demanding test of sustained physical effort, and her first-place finish adds another achievement to her continuing participation in fitness and sporting activities.

Her accomplishment aptly reflects the spirit of “Athletes Are Made, Not Born”, highlighting the value of regular physical activity, determination and sustained effort across the years.

TTPA congratulates Dr. R. Asha Rajini on securing First Prize in the MEPZ Half Marathon and wishes her continued success in her sporting pursuits.
