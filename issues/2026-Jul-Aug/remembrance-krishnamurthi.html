Dr. M. Krishnamurthi

In Fond Remembrance

Dr. M. Krishnamurthi, former Associate Professor of Veterinary Clinical Medicine at VCRI, Namakkal, passed away in Canada on 25 June 2026.

After taking voluntary retirement, he settled in Canada. During the formative years of TANUVAS, he maintained cordial relations with leaders of the ruling party and effectively leveraged them for the University's benefit.

Along with his wife, he instituted the "Dr. M. Krishnamurthi and Mrs. Jayanthi Krishnamurthi Gold Medal" for the highest scorer in Veterinary Medicine at VCRI, Namakkal.

He had been ailing for some time, and his last rites were performed in Canada on 28 June 2026.

We recall Dr. Krishnamurthi expressing immense delight upon seeing his esteemed colleagues, highlighting the enduring bonds of friendship within our veterinary community during his convalescing period at a Chennai hospital.

TTPA extends its heartfelt condolences to the bereaved family and remembers Dr. Krishnamurthi with respect and affection.
