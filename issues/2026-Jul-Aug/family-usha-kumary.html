Welcome to Dr. S. Usha Kumary

The TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association (TTPA) warmly welcomes Dr. S. Usha Kumary, who retired after rendering more than 33 years of dedicated service to TANUVAS.

A distinguished veterinary anatomist, she served in various academic positions at VCRI Namakkal, Madras Veterinary College, Chennai, and VCRI Salem, eventually retiring as Professor and Head, Department of Veterinary Anatomy, Madras Veterinary College.

An accomplished teacher, researcher, and innovator, she received the Chief Minister’s Award for the Best M.V.Sc. Thesis and the SKM Gold Medal for the Best Ph.D. Thesis.

Her notable contributions include developing the “Virtual Dissection of Calf” digital tool and mobile learning applications.

TTPA wishes her a healthy, happy, and fulfilling retired life.
