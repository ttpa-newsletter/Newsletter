Dr. Ganesh T. N. Completes 5K Fun Run at REX '26

TTPA is delighted to congratulate Dr. Ganesh T. N. on successfully completing the 5K Fun Run held during the Ruminant Expo 2026 (REX '26'), organised by the Faculty of Veterinary Medicine, University Malaysia Kelantan, as part of the UMK Veterinary Business & Entrepreneurship Symposium (VBES 2.0) on 15 May 2026.

Adding special significance to his achievement, Dr. Ganesh proudly wore a T-shirt bearing the Madras Veterinary College logo throughout the run, reflecting his enduring affection and pride for his alma mater.

TTPA applauds his commitment to fitness, international participation, and unwavering institutional spirit and wishes him many more such accomplishments.
