Dr. R. Prabakaran Addresses Global Veterinary Challenges and Opportunities

Dr. R. Prabakaran delivered an insightful online guest lecture on 07 May 2026 during the 1st International Conference on Veterinary & Animal Husbandry Sciences (ICVAHS 2026), jointly organized by the Apollo College of Veterinary Medicine (Jaipur), the ISVAHR Society, and the Just Veterinary Education Group.

The conference focused on “Emerging Trends & Advanced Technologies in Veterinary & Animal Husbandry Sciences: Challenges & Opportunities.” Dr. Prabakaran lectured on the theme of the conference and emphasized the importance of integrating modern technologies with field-level applications to strengthen livestock production, disease control, and global food security in the coming decades.

The gathering featured keynotes and sessions from prominent experts, including officials from the Veterinary Council of India (VCI) and various state universities.

TTPA congratulates Dr. R. Prabakaran on his contribution to this important international academic programme.
