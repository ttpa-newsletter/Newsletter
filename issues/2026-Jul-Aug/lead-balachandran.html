Called to Lead Once More

Dr. C. Balachandran

Dr. C. Balachandran Elected as President of the Indian College of Veterinary Pathologists (ICVP)

The TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association is delighted to congratulate Dr. C. Balachandran on his election as President of the Indian College of Veterinary Pathologists (ICVP) for a two-year term commencing in 2026.

Established in 2008, ICVP is the premier board-certifying body for veterinary pathologists in India, conducting rigorous examinations leading to Diplomate status.

The certification recognizes advanced professional competence and promotes excellence in veterinary pathology, research, diagnostics, and education.

Dr. Balachandran’s election to this prestigious position reflects his outstanding contributions to the profession. TTPA extends its heartfelt best wishes for a successful and productive tenure.
