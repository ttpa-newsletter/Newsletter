Dr. S. Damodaran

M.V.Sc.

Former Professor of Pathology
Madras Veterinary College

An Exceptional Educator and Mentor

Dr. S. Damodaran was an exceptional educator and a beloved mentor whose passion for teaching and deep commitment to his students left an indelible mark on generations of veterinary professionals.

He joined Madras Veterinary College in the 1950s and began his academic career as a Lecturer in Anatomy.

His patient and approachable teaching style made him a favourite among students who sought clarity, guidance and encouragement.

Transition to Veterinary Pathology

His academic career subsequently took him to the Department of Pathology, where he became a Reader and established himself as a demanding but deeply committed teacher.

He was remembered as a true taskmaster who combined discipline with encouragement and instilled in his students both academic rigour and a genuine love for learning.

Legendary Teaching Methods

Dr. Damodaran's teaching methods became legendary among his students.

He would spend long hours guiding students through microscopic slides and pathology atlases, ensuring that each lesion, structure and pathological concept was understood thoroughly.

His approach emphasised careful observation, interpretation and correlation rather than mere memorisation.

Teaching Beyond the Classroom

His commitment to students did not end when formal classes were over.

He spent evenings reviewing student reports and provided candid, constructive feedback that promoted both academic excellence and self-confidence.

Even bus journeys and casual walks could become opportunities for discussion and learning.

For Dr. Damodaran, mentorship was not confined to a timetable; it was a continuing relationship with his students.

Building Confidence Through Constructive Guidance

One of his notable qualities was his ability to combine strict academic standards with personal encouragement.

His criticism was candid but constructive. Students understood that his insistence on accuracy and discipline arose from a sincere desire to see them improve.

Through this approach he helped nurture not only competent pathologists but also confident veterinary professionals.

Contribution to Professional Pathology

Dr. Damodaran's influence extended beyond Madras Veterinary College.

He played an important role in the founding and development of the Indian Association of Veterinary Pathologists.

His encouragement and participation helped the organisation grow into an important scientific platform for veterinary pathologists.

Mentor to Future Leaders

Many of the students who benefited from his teaching and mentorship subsequently developed distinguished careers in veterinary education, research and professional organisations.

Some went on to assume leadership roles in the very professional association that he had helped to nurture.

This represented one of the most enduring dimensions of his contribution: the development of people who would carry his academic values forward.

Humility, Kindness and Magnanimity

Colleagues and students remembered Dr. Damodaran as a soft-spoken and unassuming personality.

Beneath his demanding academic standards was a deeply kind and considerate individual who genuinely cared about the growth and success of his students.

His humility, magnanimity and willingness to support others set a high standard for academic mentorship.

More Than a Teacher

To those who studied under him, Dr. Damodaran was more than a professor.

He was a guide, mentor and source of inspiration whose wisdom and encouragement often influenced important academic and professional decisions.

His students valued not only what he taught them about pathology, but also what they learnt from him about discipline, sincerity and responsibility.

An Enduring Legacy

Dr. S. Damodaran's legacy lives on in the many veterinary professionals whose careers he helped shape.

His dedication to teaching, compassion for students and insistence on academic rigour continue to represent the highest ideals of veterinary education.

He remains an inspiration to all who had the privilege of learning from him—a true Guru whose wisdom and guidance continue to illuminate the path for future generations.

Long live his glory.

Biography by Dr. A. Rajan, Dean (Retd.), Kerala Agricultural University, College of Veterinary and Animal Sciences, Mannuthy; alumnus of Madras Veterinary College.
