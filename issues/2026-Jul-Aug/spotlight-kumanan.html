Pensioners in the Spotlight

Dr. K. Kumanan

Dr. K. Kumanan Appointed to the Technical Core Committee

Dr. K. Kumanan has earned the distinction of being appointed to the Technical Core Committee constituted by the Government of India to tackle Highly Pathogenic Avian Influenza (HPAI).

This distinguished recognition reflects his outstanding expertise, professional excellence, and unwavering commitment to veterinary science and public health.

At a time when the nation is strengthening its biosafety measures and disease preparedness, Dr. Kumanan’s technical acumen and leadership will play a pivotal role in safeguarding animal health, protecting the poultry sector, and supporting national disease-control strategies.

TTPA takes immense pride in this achievement and wishes him every success in this important national responsibility.
