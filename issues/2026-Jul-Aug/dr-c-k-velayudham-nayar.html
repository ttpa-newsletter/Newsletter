Dr. C. K. Velayudham Nayar

G.M.V.C., M.S. (USA)

Professor of Pharmacology
Madras Veterinary College

Early Academic Career

Dr. C. K. Velayudham Nayar presumably joined the faculty of the Department of Pharmacology and Toxicology, Madras Veterinary College, during 1939–1940.

At that time, Madras Veterinary College was providing veterinary education for the erstwhile Madras Presidency, an extensive geographical region presently covering parts of several states.

An Outstanding Teacher

As a teacher, Dr. Nayar was distinguished by his dedication, sincerity, command of English, devotion to teaching and passion for his students.

These qualities earned him recognition as one of the outstanding teachers of his time.

His reputation extended beyond Madras Veterinary College. Some medical colleges in the city invited him to deliver guest lectures, recognising his academic merit and teaching ability.

Development of Postgraduate Pharmacology

The Department was upgraded to offer postgraduate studies in Pharmacology during 1960–1961.

Dr. Nayar was recognised to impart postgraduate education and guide research in the discipline.

This was an era when research technology and instrumentation were far less advanced than those available today.

Research with the Tools of an Earlier Era

Despite the technological limitations of the period, Dr. Nayar effectively guided postgraduate students in their thesis research using the experimental facilities then available.

These included classical pharmacological apparatus such as the kymograph and isolated organ bath, together with experimental animal models.

Such techniques formed the foundation of experimental pharmacology and enabled students to understand drug responses through direct observation and physiological recording.

Mentor to Generations of Researchers

Dr. Nayar is remembered as having guided a large number of postgraduate scholars in their research.

It is recalled that he may have guided more than 50 scholars, many of whom subsequently distinguished themselves as researchers.

His contribution therefore extended far beyond classroom teaching; through his students, he helped build a strong academic and research tradition in Veterinary Pharmacology.

A Teacher and Mentor Remembered

I had the privilege of being his student and receiving his mentorship during my postgraduate studies from 1965 to 1967.

I was also fortunate to work under his stewardship for two years during 1968–1970.

These years gave me an opportunity to observe closely his commitment to teaching, research and the development of younger faculty members.

Retirement and Enduring Contribution

Dr. Nayar retired from service around 1969–1970, leaving behind a rich academic legacy.

His services to the Department and the Faculty were outstanding and commendable. His contributions deserve to be remembered and made known to younger generations of veterinarians.

“Father of Pharmacology”

Through his pioneering teaching, postgraduate education, research guidance and mentorship, Dr. C. K. Velayudham Nayar played a formative role in establishing the tradition of Veterinary Pharmacology at Madras Veterinary College.

He is remembered by members of Madras Veterinary College as the “Father of Pharmacology.”

Reminiscence by Professor B. Gowrisankar (Retd.), Department of Veterinary Pharmacology and Toxicology.
