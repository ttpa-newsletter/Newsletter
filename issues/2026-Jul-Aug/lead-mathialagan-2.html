Research Proposal Selection Committee Member

Dr. P. Mathialagan

Dr. P. Mathialagan has been nominated as an Expert Member of the Selection Committee

The TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association (TTPA) is delighted to learn that Dr. P. Mathialagan has been nominated as an Expert Member of the Selection Committee for reviewing proposals under the Student Project Scheme in Veterinary Sciences.

Student projects play a vital role in nurturing scientific inquiry, innovation, critical thinking, and research skills among young veterinary scholars. The selection of meritorious projects helps promote quality research and encourages students to address emerging challenges in animal health and production.

TTPA considers this nomination a fitting recognition of Dr. Mathialagan’s expertise and academic standing and extends its heartfelt congratulations and best wishes for the successful discharge of this important responsibility.
