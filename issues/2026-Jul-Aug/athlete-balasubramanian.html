Dr. S. Balasubramanian Celebrates International Yoga Day

The TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association (TTPA) warmly congratulates Dr. S. Balasubramanian on his inspiring observance of the 12th International Yoga Day on 21 June 2026. Through the practice of various yoga postures, he has demonstrated the importance of maintaining physical fitness, mental well-being, and inner balance.

Yoga is particularly beneficial for senior citizens, helping improve flexibility, balance, strength, respiratory efficiency, and stress management. Regular practice can reduce the risk of lifestyle-related disorders and promote healthy ageing, enabling older adults to lead active and independent lives.

Dr. Balasubramanian's commitment to yoga serves as a motivating example for fellow pensioners and veterinary professionals alike. TTPA appreciates his dedication and extends its heartfelt congratulations and best wishes.
