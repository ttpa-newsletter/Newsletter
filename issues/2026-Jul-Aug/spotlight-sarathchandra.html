Pensioners in the Spotlight

Dr. G. Sarathchandra

Textbook on Food Toxicology by Dr. G. Sarathchandra

TTPA proudly congratulates Dr. G. Sarathchandra on the publication of his Textbook on Food Toxicology, released under the auspices of the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR).

The book gained national recognition when it was officially launched by the Hon’ble Prime Minister of India on 28 April, marking a significant milestone for the author and the scientific community.

Written in a clear and comprehensive manner, the textbook examines food-borne toxins, contaminants, safety concerns, risk assessment, and regulatory aspects affecting public health.

By integrating contemporary scientific knowledge with practical applications, it serves as a valuable resource for students, researchers, veterinarians, and food safety professionals.

This noteworthy contribution strengthens awareness of food safety and toxicological sciences.
