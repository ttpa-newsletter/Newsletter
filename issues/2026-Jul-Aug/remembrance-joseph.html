Dr. S. A. Joseph

In Fond Remembrance

Dr. S. A. Joseph expired on 6 June 2026.

He was the senior-most surviving veterinarian and a distinguished parasitologist as well as an inspiring teacher to generations of students at Madras Veterinary College.

He earned his Ph.D. from the University of Oklahoma, specialising in the study of fleas, particularly those affecting poultry.

His contributions to teaching and research have been fondly remembered and highly appreciated by his students, colleagues, seniors, and juniors alike.

He served as Professor of Veterinary Parasitology at Madras Veterinary College from 1990 to 1993 and retired in 1993. His informative booklet on the fleas of birds and animals continues to be regarded as a valuable reference in the department.

A kind-hearted, soft-spoken, and true gentleman veterinarian, he made immense contributions to veterinary parasitology and had the opportunity to work alongside George C. Ewing, after whom the rickettsial organism Ehrlichia ewingii infection was named.

His legacy as a teacher, researcher, and mentor continues to inspire the veterinary fraternity.

On behalf of TTPA and the Department of Parasitology, Dr. Gomathinayagam, Dr. G. Rajavelu, and Dr. B. R. Latha paid their last tributes to the departed soul.

TTPA condoles his demise and offers its heartfelt condolences to his bereaved family and colleagues.
