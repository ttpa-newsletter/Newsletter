Dr. T. Sivakumar

In Fond Remembrance

Dr. T. Sivakumar, Professor and Head (Retd.), LPM, MVC, former Dean, VCRI, Orathanadu and former Dean, CFDT, Koduveli, expired on 13 May 2026.

With profound sorrow, the TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association (TTPA) mourns the untimely demise of Dr. T. Sivakumar.

Born on 24 June 1962, he was an outstanding scholar who earned his B.V.Sc. (1986), M.V.Sc. (1988), Ph.D. (1995), M.Sc. in Animal and Forage Science from the University of Reading (1997), and a Postgraduate Diploma in Education Technology.

A recipient of the Dr. A.L. Mudaliar Award for Overall Best Performance in M.V.Sc., he excelled as a teacher, researcher, extension specialist, and institution builder.

During his distinguished TANUVAS career, he guided numerous postgraduate scholars, secured over ₹501 crore in research funding, received the prestigious TANSA Best Scientist Award, and played a pivotal role in strengthening CFDT, Koduveli, and VCRI, Orathanadu.

On behalf of TTPA, floral tributes were paid by Dr. Gomathynayagam, Dr. Gunaseelan, Dr. V. Balakrishnan, and several other pensioners.

TTPA respectfully offers its heartfelt condolences to his bereaved family and colleagues.
