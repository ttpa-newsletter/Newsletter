Called to Lead Once More

Dr. Dhanan Jaya Rao

Dr. (Capt.) G. Dhanan Jaya Rao Elected as Secretary General of Veterinary Association of India

The TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association (TTPA) is pleased to congratulate Dr. (Capt.) G. Dhanan Jaya Rao on his election as Secretary General of the Veterinary Association of India (VAI).

As one of the leading professional organizations representing veterinarians across the country, VAI plays a vital role in promoting animal health, advancing veterinary education and research, strengthening professional standards, and addressing issues of national importance related to livestock and public health.

Dr. Rao’s election to this prestigious office reflects the confidence reposed in his leadership and professional expertise.

TTPA extends its heartfelt best wishes and hopes for a highly successful and productive tenure.
