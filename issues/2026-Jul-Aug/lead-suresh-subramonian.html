Called to Lead Once More

Dr. B. Suresh Subramonian

Dr. B. Suresh Subramonian Appointed Dean, School of Veterinary and Animal Sciences, Odisha

TTPA takes pride in congratulating Dr. B. Suresh Subramonian on his appointment as Dean, School of Veterinary and Animal Sciences (SoVAS), Centurion University of Technology and Management, Odisha, with effect from 15 May 2026.

Having efficiently served as Professor and In-charge Dean, Dr. Subramonian has now been formally designated as Dean in recognition of his leadership, academic contributions, and commitment to veterinary education.

He will continue to shoulder his responsibilities as Head, Department of Livestock Product Technology.

His elevation reflects the confidence reposed in his administrative capabilities and vision for advancing veterinary education, research, and professional excellence.

We wish him continued success in this prestigious position.

The appointment was announced through an official circular issued by the Registrar of Centurion University on 15 May 2026.
