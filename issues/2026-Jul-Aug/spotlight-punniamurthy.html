Padma Shri Dr. N. Punniamurthy

Padma Shri Dr. N. Punniamurthy Delivers Convocation Address at Kamdhenu University

Padma Shri Dr. N. Punniamurthy delivered the Convocation Address as the Chief Guest at the Twelfth Annual Convocation of Kamdhenu University, Gandhinagar, Gujarat.

Addressing the graduating students, he urged them to uphold professional ethics, compassion, and lifelong learning while serving society.

He emphasised the importance of the One Health approach, sustainable livestock production, and the integration of ethnoveterinary knowledge with modern science to address emerging global challenges.

He further inspired young veterinarians to embrace innovation and entrepreneurship in service of animal welfare, public health, and environmental sustainability.

TTPA extends warm congratulations to Dr. Punniamurthy and wishes him continued success in guiding the next generation of veterinary professionals.
