Dr. R. Karunakaran

Welcome to Dr. R. Karunakaran

The TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association (TTPA) extends a warm and heartfelt welcome to Dr. R. Karunakaran, former Professor and Head, Department of Animal Nutrition, Madras Veterinary College, Chennai, who retired on superannuation on 30 June 2026 after more than 33 years of dedicated service.

He was earlier Dean, Madras Veterinary College, Chennai. A recipient of the prestigious Dr. K.P.C. Nair Best Teacher Award and former Dean of Madras Veterinary College, Dr. Karunakaran has made notable contributions to animal nutrition research, particularly in probiotics and nanomineral technologies.

An accomplished teacher, researcher, mentor, and administrator, he has guided numerous students and enriched veterinary education through his scholarly, extension, and innovative initiatives.

TTPA wishes him a healthy, happy, and fulfilling retired life.
