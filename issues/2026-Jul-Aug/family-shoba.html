Welcome to Dr. K. Shoba

The TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association (TTPA) extends a warm and heartfelt welcome to Dr. K. Shoba, who retired as Professor and Head, Department of Veterinary Microbiology, Madras Veterinary College, Chennai, after more than 33 years of dedicated service to TANUVAS.

A distinguished alumna of Madras Veterinary College, she earned her B.V.Sc., M.V.Sc., and Ph.D. degrees with exceptional academic distinction, winning nine gold medals across her academic career.

She was also honored with the Young Woman Scientist Award in 2001 by Science City, Government of Tamil Nadu.

A committed teacher and researcher, Dr. Shoba has published 40 scientific papers in reputed journals.

TTPA wishes her a healthy, happy, and fulfilling retired life.
