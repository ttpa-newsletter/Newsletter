Newsletter Format

Objectives, format, guidelines and article submission

Newsletter Objectives

  • To disseminate the activities of the TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association.
  • To spread awareness about old age-related information.
  • To share pensioners' or wards' achievements recognised by a third party from the preceding edition onwards.

Newsletter Format

  • Greeting message by the President or General Secretary on national or international professional days.
  • Minutes of the Executive Council meeting.
  • Introduce newcomers.
  • A joyful rewind of memories and interaction with colleagues and students by TTPA members as teachers.
  • New professional information.
  • Abstract of Geriatric Tips for a Healthy Life.
  • Pensioner achievement during the reporting period.
  • Pensioner philanthropic activity during the reporting period.
  • Laudable act of a pensioner.
  • Events organised by members at their location.
  • Events participated in by members organised by an alma mater, private, state, national or international body.
  • Tips on savings and their security.
  • Reunion photos.
  • Appreciating a deceased colleague / Obituary.
  • Letters to the Editor.

Prohibited Articles in the Newsletter

  • Appreciation or condemnation of a living pensioner.
  • Religious messages.
  • Political messages.
  • Grievances against the University or Government.

Article Submission

Members can send articles directly with relevant photos through WhatsApp.
