Welcome to Dr. V. Appa Rao

The TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association (TTPA) warmly welcomes Dr. V. Appa Rao, former Professor and Head, Department of Livestock Products Technology (Meat Science), Madras Veterinary College, Chennai, former Dean, College of Food and Dairy Technology, Alamathi - Koduvalli, Chennai - 600 052.

A distinguished teacher, researcher, administrator, and extension specialist, Dr. Appa Rao rendered more than three decades of dedicated service to veterinary and food sciences education.

Renowned for his innovations in meat processing technologies, infrastructure development, and academic leadership, he has guided numerous postgraduate and doctoral scholars and published extensively.

A recipient of several prestigious awards, including the ICAR Director General’s Commendation Award, he has significantly contributed to research, extension, and institutional growth.

TTPA wishes him a healthy, happy, and rewarding retired life.
