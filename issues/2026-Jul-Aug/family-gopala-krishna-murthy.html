Welcome to Dr. T. R. Gopala Krishna Murthy

The TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association (TTPA) extends a warm and cordial welcome to Dr. T. R. Gopala Krishna Murthy, distinguished veterinary microbiologist and former Professor and Head, Poultry Disease Diagnosis and Surveillance Laboratory, VCRI, Namakkal.

With nearly three decades of dedicated service, he has made outstanding contributions to poultry health, disease diagnosis, epidemiological surveillance, research, and veterinary education.

Renowned for his pioneering work on emerging poultry pathogens and molecular diagnostics, he has significantly strengthened poultry disease management in one of India's largest poultry production regions.

A prolific researcher, mentor, and recipient of the 2026 Lifetime Achievement Award, Dr. Murthy has brought great distinction to TANUVAS and the veterinary profession.

TTPA wishes him a healthy, happy, and fulfilling retired life.
