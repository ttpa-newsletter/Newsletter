Dr. G. Dhanan Jaya Rao Moderates International Webinar with Distinction

The TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association (TTPA) congratulates Dr. (Capt.) G. Dhanan Jaya Rao for his exemplary role as Moderator of the international webinar, “Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam and One Health: Yogic Principles for Human and Animal Welfare,” organized by the Veterinary Association of India on 21 June 2026.

The webinar highlighted the importance of integrating yogic principles with the One Health concept to promote the well-being of humans, animals, and the environment. Serving as a moderator in an international forum is a demanding responsibility that requires thorough subject knowledge, effective communication, time management, and the ability to coordinate speakers and audience interactions seamlessly. Dr. Rao discharged these responsibilities with professionalism and grace.

TTPA extends its heartfelt congratulations and best wishes for his continued success.
