Welcome to Dr. S. Meenakshi Sundaram

The TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association warmly welcomes Dr. S. Meenakshi Sundaram, former Professor and Head, Livestock Farm Complex, TANUVAS, Chennai, and former Director, Centre for Animal Production Studies.

A distinguished academician, researcher, administrator, and extension specialist, he served the University with dedication for over 25 years.

He made significant contributions to teaching, research, extension, and institutional development through successful project leadership, postgraduate guidance, and promotion of innovative livestock technologies.

His tireless efforts for the welfare of farmers and the livestock sector earned him wide recognition and respect.

TTPA is honoured to have such an accomplished professional among its members and wishes him good health, happiness, and a rewarding association with the fraternity.
