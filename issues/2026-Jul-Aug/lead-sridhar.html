Called to Lead Once More

Dr. S. Sridhar

Dr. R. Sridhar Recognised as Expert and Lectured to Faculty

TTPA congratulates Dr. R. Sridhar on his nomination as an Expert Member of the Selection Committee constituted by the West Bengal University of Animal and Fishery Sciences for the promotion of teachers to the post of Associate Professor on 17 June 2026.

The following day, he delivered an invited lecture to faculty members, postgraduate scholars, and undergraduate students of the Veterinary College, Kolkata, on “Demystifying Wildlife Post-mortem Examination: An Eye-opener for Undergraduates.”

His participation reflects the high regard for his professional expertise and his commitment to advancing veterinary education.

TTPA extends its warm congratulations and wishes him continued success in his academic endeavours.
