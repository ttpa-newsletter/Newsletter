Pensioners in the Spotlight

Dr. R. Prabakaran

Dr. R. Prabakaran Speaks on the Power of Human Values and Organisational Culture in Malaysia

Dr. R. Prabakaran delivered an inspiring keynote address on “The Power of Human Values and Organisation Culture” during the Annual Meeting of Neotle Global Private Limited held at Hotel Mercure, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, on 22 April 2026.

The lecture emphasised the importance of ethics, integrity, mutual respect, and positive workplace culture in achieving sustainable organisational growth and social progress.

He highlighted that value-based leadership enhances teamwork, employee well-being, and institutional harmony.

The session gained significance in today’s rapidly changing professional environment, where human-centred organisational practices are essential.

Senior executives, managers, technical professionals, and young corporate personnel actively participated in the interactive programme.

TTPA congratulates Dr. R. Prabakaran on this significant contribution.
