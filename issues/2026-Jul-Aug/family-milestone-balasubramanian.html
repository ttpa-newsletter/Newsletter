Dr. S. Balasubramanian’s Daughter Wedding

Celebrating a joyous family milestone

The marriage of Selvi Lavanya Balasubramanian, B.Tech., M.Sc. (UK), R&D Engineer, Curtiss-Wright, UK, with Vignesh Rajan, B.Tech., M.Sc. (UK), Project Engineer, AMRC, UK, was solemnised on 17 June 2026 at Sri Rama Kalyana Mandapam, 24 Cathedral Garden Road, Nungambakkam, Chennai – 600034.

The ceremony was conducted in a traditional and graceful manner in the presence of family members and well-wishers. The occasion marked a joyous union of two accomplished individuals.

TTPA conveys its heartfelt blessings to the newly married couple, wishing them a happy, harmonious, and prosperous married life.
