Age is just a number – fitness is a habit

Age is just a number – fitness is a habit

Role models: Asha & Kathiresan

This cartoon celebrates the spirit of fitness, vitality and active ageing, reminding us that age need not be a barrier to maintaining an active and healthy lifestyle.

The example of Asha and Kathiresan reminds us that regular physical activity, enthusiasm and a positive outlook can inspire people of all generations.
