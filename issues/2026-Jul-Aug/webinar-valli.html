Dr. C. Valli Delivers Insightful Webinar on Climate-Resilient Forage Systems

The TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association (TTPA) congratulates Dr. C. Valli for her excellent webinar presentation on “Climate-Resilient Fodder Crops, Pasture Species, and Forage Diversification Strategies for Integrated Systems,” organized by the Department of Animal Nutrition, College of Veterinary and Animal Sciences, Kishanganj, Bihar Animal Sciences University (BASU), Patna.

The topic is of immense significance in the face of climate change, as sustainable fodder production is crucial for ensuring livestock productivity, feed security, and environmental resilience. Dr. Valli’s presentation was highly engaging, and her ability to explain complex scientific concepts with clarity and practical relevance made the session truly impactful.

TTPA warmly congratulates Dr. Valli and wishes her continued success in advancing livestock nutrition and sustainable agriculture.
