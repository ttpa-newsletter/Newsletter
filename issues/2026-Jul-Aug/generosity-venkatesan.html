Dr. A. M. Venkatesan TANUVAS Premier League – Champions Rolling Trophy 2026

The TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association (TTPA) proudly acknowledges the generous sponsorship of the TANUVAS Premier League – Champions Rolling Trophy cricket tournament by Dr. A. M. Venkatesan, Deputy Director of Physical Education (Retired).

The tournament was successfully conducted at VCRI, Orathanadu, from 6th to 10th May 2026. The event brought together enthusiastic participants from various constituent units of TANUVAS, fostering sportsmanship, teamwork, and camaraderie among faculty, staff, and students.

The tournament provided an excellent platform for showcasing athletic talent and strengthening institutional bonds. The spirited competition, disciplined conduct, and enthusiastic participation made the event a memorable celebration of sports and unity within the TANUVAS community.

TTPA expresses its sincere appreciation to Dr. A. M. Venkatesan for his generous support and contribution towards making the event a memorable success.
