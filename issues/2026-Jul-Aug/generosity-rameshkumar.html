Dr. B. Rameshkumar at the Rotary International Convention 2026

Rtn. Major Donor Dr. B. Rameshkumar represented Rotary District 3233 at the Rotary International Convention held in Taipei, Taiwan, from 13–17 June 2026, which was attended by nearly 37,000 Rotarians from around the world.

He participated in deliberations at the House of Friendship on Rotary's seven areas of focus, including peacebuilding, disease prevention, water and sanitation, maternal and child health, education, community development, and environmental sustainability.

He also exchanged the Rotary Club of Madras Mount flag with fellow Rotary clubs worldwide.

TTPA congratulates Dr. Rameshkumar on his active international participation and wishes him continued success in humanitarian service.
