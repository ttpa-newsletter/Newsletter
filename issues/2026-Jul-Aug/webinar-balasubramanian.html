Dr. S. Balasubramanian Organised Global Veterinary Clinical Sciences Webinar 2026

The TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association (TTPA) is delighted to congratulate Dr. S. Balasubramanian on the resounding success of the Global Veterinary Clinical Sciences Webinar 2026 held on 30 May 2026.

The webinar featured an enlightening lecture on “Anaesthetic and Analgesic Management of the Brachycephalic Dog” by Prof. Dr. Kirk Munoz, Assistant Professor, Ohio State University, USA, and Board-Certified Specialist in Veterinary Anaesthesia and Analgesia.

The topic was highly relevant, as brachycephalic breeds present unique anaesthetic challenges requiring specialized perioperative care and pain management.

The overwhelming response, with 1,369 registrations and 1,243 online participants, reflects the global interest in this important subject.

TTPA warmly congratulates Dr. Balasubramanian and his team for organizing this outstanding academic event.
