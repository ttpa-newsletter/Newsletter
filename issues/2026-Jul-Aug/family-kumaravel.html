Welcome to Dr. A. Kumaravel

The TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association (TTPA) extends a warm and heartfelt welcome to Dr. A. Kumaravel, who retired on 30 June 2026 as Professor and Head, Department of Veterinary Anatomy, VCRI Namakkal.

Dr. Kumaravel is a distinguished academic whose accomplishments extend far beyond veterinary education. In addition to his MVSc and Ph.D. qualifications, he has earned three Master's degrees in Tamil, English, and Philosophy—an extraordinary achievement that reflects his passion for lifelong learning.

A gifted orator, writer, and scholar, he is widely recognized in Tamil literary circles for his contributions to literature, science communication, and devotional studies.

His popular television programs on SUN TV, Makkal TV, Pothigai TV, and Polimer TV have brought scientific and cultural knowledge to diverse audiences.

He has authored acclaimed books, published award-winning short stories and poetry, and regularly contributed articles to leading Tamil publications.
