Dr. Asha Rajini Achieves Remarkable Podium Finish at TCS World 10K Bengaluru

The TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners Association (TTPA) proudly congratulates Dr. Asha Rajini on her outstanding achievement of securing second place and a podium finish in the prestigious TCS World 10 Km Bengaluru. This internationally recognized road race promotes fitness, endurance, and healthy living while inspiring people of all ages to embrace an active lifestyle.

What makes this accomplishment truly exceptional is that Dr. Asha Rajini achieved this remarkable feat at the age of 71. Her determination, resilience, and positive spirit are a powerful example of the benefits of perseverance, an active lifestyle, and a commitment to fitness.

She stands as an inspiration to senior citizens everywhere. TTPA extends its heartfelt congratulations and wishes her continued health, happiness, and success.
