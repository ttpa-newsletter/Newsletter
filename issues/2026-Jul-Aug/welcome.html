Welcome to the July–August 2026 Issue

Dear Members,

It gives me great pleasure to welcome you to the July–August 2026 issue of the TTPA Multimedia Newsletter.

This issue brings together the activities, achievements, memories and contributions of the members of the TANUVAS Teachers Pensioners' Association.

The newsletter provides an opportunity for our pensioners' community to remain connected, informed and engaged with one another.

We hope that this issue will bring back pleasant memories, highlight meaningful contributions and keep our community closely connected.

I wish all our members and their families good health, happiness and continued fellowship.
