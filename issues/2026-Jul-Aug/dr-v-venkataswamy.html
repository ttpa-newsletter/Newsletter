Reminiscence of Dedicated Stalwarts

Remembering those who served with distinction.

Dr. V. Venkataswamy

B.A., B.V.Sc., M.S., Ph.D. (USA)

Professor and Head
Department of Obstetrics and Gynaecology
Madras Veterinary College

Early Life and Education

Dr. V. Venkataswamy was born on 10 May 1925 to Shri Vardhaiya Naidu and Shrimathi Kantha Ammal in the village of Melpati in North Arcot District, Tamil Nadu.

He came from a humble farming family and, despite financial constraints, persevered through various odd jobs to support his education. He graduated with a B.A. from Madanapalli and subsequently earned his B.V.Sc. degree from Madras Veterinary College, University of Madras, in 1950.

Academic Career and Higher Studies

After graduation, Dr. Venkataswamy joined the faculty of Madras Veterinary College and served until 1954. He then proceeded to the United States for higher studies.

In 1954, he enrolled in a postgraduate-cum-doctoral programme at the University of Minnesota, where he pursued his studies with dedication and earned his doctorate in 1958.

Returning to India in 1959, he resumed his academic career at Madras Veterinary College. He served in several capacities, including Lecturer in Genetics and Reader in Dairy Science.

In 1966, he became Professor and Head of the Department of Obstetrics and Gynaecology, a position he held until 1977.

At a time when very few faculty members possessed doctoral qualifications, he guided and mentored veterinarians from across India in the field of veterinary obstetrics and gynaecology.

Dean of Madras Veterinary College

Dr. Venkataswamy occupied the prestigious position of Dean, Madras Veterinary College, from 1977 to 1978.

His academic leadership and wide-ranging experience contributed significantly to the development of veterinary education and professional training.

International Contribution

Dr. Venkataswamy's expertise extended beyond India. He was appointed as a World Bank Expert in livestock production and played a pivotal role in the development of the Malaysian Agricultural Research and Development Institute (MARDI) from 1978 to 1980.

Following the completion of his tenure at MARDI, he returned to Madras Veterinary College and resumed his position as Professor and Head of Obstetrics and Gynaecology until his retirement in 1983.

During this period, he also served as Warden of the Madras Veterinary College Hostel.

Recognition and Professional Contribution

Throughout his career, Dr. Venkataswamy's contributions as an educator, researcher and mentor were widely acclaimed.

In 1990, he was nominated as a member of the expert committee on Veterinary & Animal Sciences Education, Research and Extension, further reflecting the recognition accorded to his expertise and experience.

Contributions Beyond the Institution

Dr. V. Venkataswamy's legacy extended well beyond his academic and professional career. His philanthropic initiatives included:

  • Establishing the Dr. V. Venkataswamy Travel Grant endowment to provide financial assistance to scholars attending conferences and workshops at TANUVAS.
  • Establishing the Sri Vallam Varadiah Naidu Memorial Award in honour of his father.
  • Establishing the Vallam Sarojini Venkataswamy Memorial Award in honour of his wife.
  • Constructing and presenting a Veterinary Dispensary to the Government of Tamil Nadu at Melpatti, his birthplace.
  • Establishing the Dr. Vallam Venkataswamy Endowment at the Government Higher Secondary School, Melpatti, providing incentives and cash awards to deserving underprivileged students.
  • Extending philanthropic support to leprosy patients as part of his commitment to social welfare.

A Legacy of Learning, Leadership and Service

Dr. V. Venkataswamy's life reflects a remarkable journey from humble beginnings to distinguished academic leadership, international professional service and enduring philanthropy.

His contributions to veterinary education, Obstetrics and Gynaecology, livestock development, mentoring and social welfare have left a lasting legacy that extends across generations.

Source material provided by Dr. C. Veerapandian, Department of Obstetrics and Gynaecology, Madras Veterinary College.
