Dr. A. M. Venkatesan Sponsors Medals for TANUVAS Sports Day Celebrations

Dr. A. M. Venkatesan, Deputy Director of Physical Education (Retd.), has once again demonstrated his unwavering commitment to promoting sports and physical fitness among the TANUVAS fraternity by generously sponsoring medals for several Sports Day celebrations across the University.

His sponsorship covers the Sports Day events scheduled at VCRI Namakkal on 23rd June, Madras Veterinary College, Chennai on 30th June, Veterinary College and Research Institute, Udumalpet on 1st July, VCRI Orathanadu on 3rd July, and VCRI Tirunelveli on 9th July, where he will also grace the occasion as Guest of Honour.

This thoughtful gesture reflects Dr. Venkatesan's enduring dedication to encouraging sporting excellence, teamwork, discipline, and healthy competition among students.

TTPA appreciates his continued support and invaluable contribution towards nurturing a vibrant sporting culture within the University.
