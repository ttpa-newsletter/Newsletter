Pensioners in the Spotlight

Dr. Dhanan Jaya Rao

Capt. (Dr.) G. Dhanan Jaya Rao Awarded the Dr. V. Kurien Lifetime Achievement Award 2026

The Tamil Nadu Veterinary Pensioners Association (TTPA) proudly congratulates Capt. (Dr.) G. Dhanan Jaya Rao, Professor and Head (Retd.), on being honored with the Dr. V. Kurien Lifetime Achievement Award 2026 by Pashudhan Praharee on the occasion of World Milk Day, 1 June 2026.

This prestigious recognition celebrates his outstanding contributions to veterinary education, dairy development, animal husbandry, and service to society over several decades.

His professional excellence, leadership, and dedication have inspired generations of veterinarians and dairy professionals.

The award is a fitting tribute to his lifelong commitment to the advancement of the livestock and dairy sectors.
