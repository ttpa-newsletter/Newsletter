Change of Address / Contact Details

Guidance for pensioners and family pensioners who move from their present residence or change their contact details.

Address & Contact Update — Quick Guide
Important: Pensioners and family pensioners who move from their current residence are advised to inform TTPA promptly of their new address and current contact details so that the Association can remain in touch, particularly during important activities such as Life Certificate submission.
